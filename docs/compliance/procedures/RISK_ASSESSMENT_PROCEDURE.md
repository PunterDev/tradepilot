# Risk Assessment Procedure

## Purpose

Define how TradePilot performs annual and significant-change risk assessments.

## Scope

Security, confidentiality, availability, billing integrity, production access, third-party vendors, customer data, application changes, and operational processes.

## Procedure

1. Review the current architecture, vendors, data inventory, incident register, vulnerability findings, access review results, and change history.
2. Identify risks to customer data, production services, billing, AI processing, and compliance controls.
3. Record risks in `docs/compliance/registers/RISK_REGISTER.md`.
4. Assign impact, likelihood, and risk rating.
5. Document existing controls and treatment plan.
6. Assign owner and target date.
7. Obtain management approval for accepted risks.
8. Reassess after major architecture, vendor, billing, security, or data-processing changes.

## Evidence

Risk register, review notes, accepted-risk approvals, remediation tickets, and management signoff.

## Frequency

At least annually and after significant changes.
