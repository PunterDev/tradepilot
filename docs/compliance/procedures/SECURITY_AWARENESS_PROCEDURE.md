# Security Awareness Procedure

## Purpose

Define practical security training for a small founder-operated SaaS.

## Scope

Applies to personnel and contractors with access to TradePilot systems, source code, production data, or customer data.

## Procedure

1. Provide security awareness training during onboarding.
2. Repeat training annually.
3. Provide additional training after significant policy changes, security incidents, or new high-risk systems.
4. Cover phishing, password security, MFA, secrets, customer data, production access, incident reporting, secure development, and social engineering.
5. Record completion using `docs/compliance/evidence/templates/SECURITY_TRAINING_RECORD.md`.
6. Restrict production access for personnel who have not completed required training unless management approves an exception.

## Evidence

Training material, completion record, date, attendee, topics, and exceptions.
