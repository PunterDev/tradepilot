# Vendor Review Procedure

## Purpose

Define how TradePilot reviews vendors that support production services or process customer data.

## Scope

Known vendors from repository evidence: GitHub, Vercel, Render, Supabase, Stripe, OpenAI, Qdrant, Databento, and Resend.

## Procedure

1. Add or update the vendor in `docs/compliance/registers/VENDOR_REGISTER.md`.
2. Identify data shared, classification, business purpose, and criticality.
3. Review available security documentation.
4. Request or download SOC 2 report, ISO certificate, security whitepaper, or equivalent where available.
5. Determine whether a DPA is required and record status.
6. Review breach/security history when available.
7. Review subprocessors and data-location commitments.
8. Verify MFA/access controls for TradePilot's vendor account.
9. Review encryption, backup, incident notification, and service availability commitments.
10. Record exit/replacement considerations.
11. Assign risk rating and next review date.

## Evidence

Completed vendor review template, vendor register entry, security report references, DPA status, and risk acceptance if needed.
