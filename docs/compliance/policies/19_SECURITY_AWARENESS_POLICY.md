# Security Awareness Policy

## Purpose

Ensure people with TradePilot access understand practical security responsibilities.

## Scope

Applies to founders, employees, contractors, and administrators with access to TradePilot systems or customer data.

## Roles and Responsibilities

- TradePilot Management approves training requirements and verifies completion.
- Personnel complete required training and report security concerns.
- Service Owners ensure vendor-specific security responsibilities are communicated.

## Policy Statements

- Security awareness training is required during onboarding.
- Refresher training is required annually.
- Additional training is required after significant policy changes or major incidents.
- Training must cover phishing, passwords, MFA, secrets, customer data, production access, incident reporting, secure development, and social engineering.

## Procedures

- Follow `docs/compliance/procedures/SECURITY_AWARENESS_PROCEDURE.md`.
- Record completion with `docs/compliance/evidence/templates/SECURITY_TRAINING_RECORD.md`.

## Evidence Retained

Training records, materials, completion dates, and exception approvals.

## Review Frequency

At least annually.

## Exceptions

Exceptions require management approval and a completion deadline.

## Enforcement

Untrained personnel should not receive or retain privileged production access without an approved exception.
