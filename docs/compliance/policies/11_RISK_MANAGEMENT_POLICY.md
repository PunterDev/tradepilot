# Risk Management Policy

## Purpose

Define how TradePilot identifies, assesses, treats, and reviews security, availability, confidentiality, vendor, and operational risks.

## Scope

Applies to production systems, customer data, source code, third-party vendors, billing operations, AI services, and compliance controls.

## Roles and Responsibilities

- TradePilot Management owns risk acceptance and prioritization.
- Service Owners identify and document risks for assigned systems.
- Application Developers identify technical risks from changes, vulnerabilities, and incidents.
- Production Administrators identify operational risks from infrastructure and access.

## Policy Statements

- TradePilot must maintain a risk register for material security and operational risks.
- Risks must include impact, likelihood, existing controls, treatment, owner, target date, status, and residual risk.
- Formal risk assessment must occur at least annually and after significant system, vendor, security, billing, or data-processing changes.
- Accepted risks require documented management approval.

## Procedures

- Use `docs/compliance/procedures/RISK_ASSESSMENT_PROCEDURE.md`.
- Maintain `docs/compliance/registers/RISK_REGISTER.md`.

## Evidence Retained

Risk register versions, annual review notes, significant-change risk reviews, accepted-risk approvals, and remediation tickets.

## Review Frequency

At least annually and after significant changes.

## Exceptions

Exceptions require management approval and residual-risk documentation.

## Enforcement

Untracked critical risks must be added to the register and assigned an owner.
