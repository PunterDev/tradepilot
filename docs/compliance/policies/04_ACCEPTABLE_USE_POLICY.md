# Acceptable Use Policy

## Purpose

Define acceptable use expectations for TradePilot systems, source code, customer data, production credentials, and vendor services.

## Scope

Applies to all TradePilot personnel, contractors, and administrators with access to company systems.

## Roles and Responsibilities

- TradePilot Management communicates and enforces acceptable use expectations.
- System Administrators monitor for misuse where practical.
- All personnel protect TradePilot systems and report suspected misuse.

## Policy Statements

- TradePilot systems may be used only for authorized business purposes.
- Customer data may not be accessed, exported, copied, or shared without legitimate support, development, legal, or security need.
- Production credentials must not be stored on personal notes, committed to repositories, shared in chat, or placed in screenshots.
- Production data must not be used in development unless explicitly approved and minimized or anonymized.
- Security controls must not be bypassed except for approved emergency containment.

## Procedures

- Report suspected misuse through the incident response process.
- Use documented production access and change management procedures for privileged work.

## Evidence Retained

Policy acknowledgments, access review records, incident records, and exception approvals.

## Review Frequency

At least annually.

## Exceptions

Exceptions require management approval and documented compensating controls.

## Enforcement

Misuse may result in access revocation, contract review, corrective action, or incident response.
