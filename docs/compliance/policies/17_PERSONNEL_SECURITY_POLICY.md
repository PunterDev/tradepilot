# Personnel Security Policy

## Purpose

Define onboarding, role changes, offboarding, and accountability expectations for people with access to TradePilot systems.

## Scope

Applies to employees, founders, contractors, advisors, and service providers with access to TradePilot systems, source code, production configuration, or customer data.

## Roles and Responsibilities

- TradePilot Management approves access and personnel security procedures.
- Production Administrators provision and revoke access.
- Service Owners confirm access needs for assigned systems.
- All personnel report suspected security issues promptly.

## Policy Statements

- Access must be granted only after business need and approval.
- Offboarding must remove access from all production and source-code systems.
- Personnel must acknowledge applicable security and acceptable-use policies.
- Security training must occur during onboarding and annually.
- One person may currently hold multiple roles due to TradePilot's size, but responsibilities must remain documented.

## Procedures

- Use access control and access review procedures for provisioning, changes, and removals.
- Use security awareness procedure for training.

## Evidence Retained

Onboarding records, access approvals, policy acknowledgments, training records, offboarding checklists, and access review records.

## Review Frequency

At least annually and after staffing/vendor changes.

## Exceptions

Exceptions require management approval and expiration.

## Enforcement

Unapproved access must be removed and reviewed.
