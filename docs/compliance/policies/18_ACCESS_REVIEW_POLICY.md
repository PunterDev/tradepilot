# Access Review Policy

## Purpose

Define periodic review requirements for production and privileged access.

## Scope

Applies to GitHub, Vercel, Render, Supabase, Stripe, OpenAI, Qdrant, Databento, Resend, domain/DNS providers, and TradePilot ADMIN accounts.

## Roles and Responsibilities

- TradePilot Management reviews and approves access review results.
- Production Administrators collect user/role evidence.
- Service Owners confirm business need for each access grant.

## Policy Statements

- Access reviews must occur at least quarterly.
- Reviews must evaluate user, role, access level, business need, MFA, and status.
- Access without current business need must be removed or reduced.
- Review evidence must not contain secrets.
- Findings and removals must be documented through completion.

## Procedures

- Follow `docs/compliance/procedures/ACCESS_REVIEW_PROCEDURE.md`.
- Use `docs/compliance/evidence/templates/ACCESS_REVIEW_TEMPLATE.md`.

## Evidence Retained

Access exports/screenshots, review template, reviewer signoff, removal/change records, and exceptions.

## Review Frequency

Quarterly.

## Exceptions

Exceptions require management approval and target review date.

## Enforcement

Unreviewed privileged access is a Type I readiness blocker.
