# TradePilot Coach External Authority Playbook

## Purpose and Guardrails

This is the operating playbook for a controlled, quality-first external-authority campaign. It is not an instruction to automate outreach or acquire backlinks at volume. Every profile, mention, review, submission, and outreach message must be accurate, relevant, and manually reviewed.

**Official brand:** TradePilot Coach  
**Canonical website:** https://www.tradepilotcoach.com  
**Positioning:** AI-powered trading journal and execution intelligence platform for futures traders.

### Truthful Product Themes

- Futures trading journal
- AI-assisted historical trade review
- Execution intelligence
- Trading behavior analysis
- Session review
- Topstep CSV import workflow
- Tradovate-compatible CSV import workflow
- Overtrading and revenge-trading analysis
- Trading discipline and process review

### Claims We Do Not Make

Never describe TradePilot Coach as providing trading signals, market predictions, guaranteed profitability, guaranteed prop-firm payouts, or guaranteed improvement. Do not imply affiliation with Topstep or Tradovate, or claim unsupported broker integrations.

## Approved Brand Description Library

Use **TradePilot Coach** in every description. Do not shorten the primary brand to only “TradePilot.”

### Short Description

TradePilot Coach is an AI trading journal for futures traders.

### Medium Description

TradePilot Coach is an AI-powered trading journal and execution intelligence platform for futures traders. Import supported CSV trade history, review sessions and behavior, and use historical analysis to identify the process patterns worth examining.

### Long Description

TradePilot Coach is an AI-powered trading journal and execution intelligence platform for futures traders. It helps traders import supported Topstep and Tradovate-compatible CSV trade history, review completed sessions, and examine recurring execution and behavior patterns. The product focuses on historical trade review, including overtrading, revenge-trading, discipline, sizing, and session context. TradePilot Coach does not provide trade signals, predict market direction, or promise trading outcomes; it helps traders use their own completed history to choose a clearer review priority.

### Product Hunt Tagline

Review your futures trading history with AI-powered execution insights.

### Founder/Maker Introduction

I built TradePilot Coach for futures traders who want a more useful review loop than a spreadsheet or blank journal entry. It turns supported completed-trade CSV history into a private workspace for reviewing sessions, execution patterns, and recurring behavior such as overtrading or trading after losses. It is not a signals product or a prediction tool. I would value practical feedback from traders on which review workflows would make their own history easier to understand.

## Quality Rules

### Good Sources

- Relevant trading and futures education sites
- Trading psychology resources
- SaaS and product directories with editorial review
- Legitimate creator descriptions and relevant podcasts
- Community resource pages
- Genuine product reviews
- Useful, authentic Reddit or community discussions

### Avoid

- Paid bulk backlink packages
- Private blog networks
- Automated guest-post networks
- Irrelevant directories
- Spun articles
- Sitewide footer-link exchanges
- Reciprocal-link schemes
- Fake reviews or fake Reddit accounts
- Automated forum posting
- Keyword-rich paid anchors

## UTM Convention for Controlled Links

Use UTMs only for links TradePilot Coach controls, including its social posts and creator partnerships. Keep editorial backlinks clean unless there is a concrete analytics reason.

```
utm_source=producthunt|youtube|x|newsletter|partner
utm_medium=referral|social|video|community
utm_campaign=phase5_authority
```

Canonical URLs remain clean; UTMs are campaign parameters, not canonical destinations.

## 30-Day Execution Plan

### Week 1

- Check brand consistency across all planned profile materials.
- Prepare the Product Hunt launch assets and factual copy.
- Submit AlternativeTo.
- Submit SaaSHub.

### Week 2

- Contact five highly relevant creators or sites.
- Naturally promote the Topstep and Tradovate export guides where they improve a resource.

### Week 3

- Contact five additional relevant targets.
- Promote the behavior and losing-session review guides.
- Follow up once with week-two contacts who have not responded.

### Week 4

- Review accepted listings.
- Verify published references.
- Check branded Google results.
- Check Google Search Console referral and search impact.
- Decide the next small set of relevant outreach targets.

Quality target: three verified software listings, five or more legitimate external mentions, and two to five editorial or niche links. There is no backlink-volume target.
